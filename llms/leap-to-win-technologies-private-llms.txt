# Propico

> Propico offers guaranteed monthly rent to property owners in India and rental protection plans to tenants in Hyderabad. We manage tenants, maintenance, and payments end-to-end.

Propico is a Hyderabad-based rental management and rental-protection service. For owners, we guarantee fixed monthly rent with zero vacancy risk. For tenants, our protection plans cover unfair deposit deductions and surprise move-out costs.
